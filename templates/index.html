🌱 CLEAN LIVING GUIDE

Small Actions. Big Impact.

Simple habits can keep our surroundings clean, protect our health and create a healthier Earth.

01

Plant More Trees

Planting trees helps improve air quality, reduce heat and pollution, provide shade and support a healthier environment.

  • Improves air quality
  • Reduces heat and pollution
  • Supports biodiversity
🍃 🍃 🍃
02

Build Recharge Pits

Recharge pits allow rainwater to enter the ground, helping replenish groundwater and reducing waterlogging during heavy rainfall.

  • Helps recharge groundwater
  • Uses rainwater effectively
  • Reduces waterlogging
💧 💧 💧
03

Follow the 3 R's

Reduce what you use, reuse items whenever possible, and recycle materials to minimize waste and conserve valuable resources.

  • Reduce unnecessary consumption
  • Reuse items whenever possible
  • Recycle useful materials
04

Segregate Waste

Separating waste at the source makes collection, recycling and processing easier and helps reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills.

  • Keep wet and dry waste separate
  • Separate recyclable materials
  • Make waste collection easier
05

Prevent Stagnant Water

Avoiding stagnant water keeps surroundings cleaner and helps prevent mosquito breeding and water-related health risks.

  • Keep drains clean and unblocked
  • Empty containers that collect water
  • Prevent mosquito breeding
