Welcome to SmartClean!

These Terms and Conditions explain the rules and responsibilities for using the SmartClean Garbage Management System. Please read them carefully before registering.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering and using SmartClean, users agree to follow these Terms and Conditions. Users who do not agree should not use the platform.

2. User Registration and Account Security

  • Users must provide accurate and genuine registration details.
  • Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials secure.
  • One person should not misuse another person's account.
  • Any suspicious account activity should be reported to the administrator.

3. Citizen Responsibilities

  • Citizens should submit genuine garbage-related complaints.
  • Complaint descriptions and uploaded photographs must be accurate.
  • Citizens should not submit false or duplicate complaints intentionally.
  • Citizens should communicate respectfully with workers and administrators.
  • Citizens may provide feedback after complaint resolution.

4. Worker Responsibilities

  • Workers should handle assigned complaints responsibly.
  • Workers must provide genuine updates about complaint progress.
  • Resolution photographs and dates must reflect actual work.
  • Workers must not falsely mark complaints as resolved.
  • Workers should maintain professional behaviour and follow safety practices.

5. Administrator Responsibilities

  • Administrators are responsible for managing users and workers.
  • Complaint assignments should be handled fairly.
  • Resolution requests should be reviewed before approval or rejection.
  • Administrators should protect confidential user information.
  • Misuse of administrative privileges is prohibited.

6. Complaint Submission Guidelines

  • Complaints must be related to garbage collection or sanitation issues.
  • Citizens should provide correct location and issue details.
  • Photographs should clearly represent the reported issue.
  • Repeated false complaints may result in account restrictions.

7. Photo Upload Guidelines

  • Uploaded photographs must be relevant to the complaint.
  • Users should not upload offensive, misleading or unrelated images.
  • Photographs should not violate another person's privacy.

8. Privacy and Data Protection

SmartClean collects necessary information to provide garbage management services. User information should be accessed only for legitimate platform purposes and should not be disclosed without proper authorization.

9. Prohibited Activities

  • Providing false registration information.
  • Uploading inappropriate or misleading content.
  • Attempting unauthorized access to the system.
  • Misusing complaint or notification facilities.
  • Harassing other users, workers or administrators.

10. Account Suspension and Termination

SmartClean administrators may restrict or suspend accounts involved in misuse, fraudulent activities or repeated violations of these Terms and Conditions, subject to applicable rules.

11. Changes to Terms and Conditions

SmartClean may update these Terms and Conditions whenever necessary. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.

12. Contact Information

For questions, complaints or technical support related to SmartClean, users can contact the system administrator.
